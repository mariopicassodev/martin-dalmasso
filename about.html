About

A niche built on detail.

I've spent the last 15 years inside music — as a performer, an arranger and a teacher. In that time I've run into the same walls again and again, in myself and in the people I work with.

Musicians who can play but haven't yet found their own voice in the music. Arrangements that look great on paper and fall apart live. Students who stall out — not for lack of talent, but because no one ever broke the concept down clearly enough.

That's exactly what I help with — arrangement, session work, original music and teaching, all built around the same idea: precision and warmth aren't opposites.

— Martín Dalmasso
