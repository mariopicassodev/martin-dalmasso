About

A niche built on detail.

Martín Dalmasso is a guitarist working at the intersection of modern jazz harmony and meticulous arrangement. His playing favors complex, carefully voiced lines and solos pursued close to perfection — the result of a deeply procedural, detail-first approach to music.

That same precision carries into how he teaches: clear, structured, and patient, breaking down harmony and improvisation so students build real, lasting understanding rather than shortcuts.
